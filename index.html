Indonesia reportedly detained an alumina cargo after rare-earth elements were detected

SMM reported that Indonesian customs had detained an alumina shipment after rare-earth elements were reportedly detected in the material. The development raises an export-verification question, but the information disclosed does not establish that all Indonesian alumina containing detectable REEs is prohibited from export.

IssueWhat the SMM article establishesWhat remains unresolvedMarket relevance
Reported detention An alumina cargo was reportedly detained after REEs were detected. The exporter, destination, HS declaration, product grade, Al₂O₃ purity, REE concentration, test method and formal legal basis were not disclosed. Confirms a cargo-level operational risk, but not an industry-wide export suspension.
Alumina classification Indonesia divides alumina into prohibited and controlled exports according to Al₂O₃ purity. Rare-earth products are listed separately. It is not clear whether detected REEs can independently override the purity classification of otherwise compliant alumina. The first eligibility test remains alumina grade and purity, not the mere detection of a trace element.
Compliant-grade exports Smelter-grade alumina at or above 98% Al₂O₃ and chemical-grade alumina at or above 90% Al₂O₃ are controlled exports requiring LS verification. Authorities have not clarified whether REE or radioactive-element testing is now a formal component of the normal LS process. Potentially increases documentation and laboratory lead time even when export eligibility is unchanged.
REE threshold The SMM review found no published finished-alumina REE threshold, testing standard or tariff-line carve-out that explains when REE content affects export status. Acceptance criteria, sampling method, retesting procedure and the treatment of trace concentrations remain unknown. Creates uncertainty over loading schedules, demurrage and replacement-cargo demand.

Alumina or rare-earth products would require a further government designation for the framework to apply directly. Should not be used alone to conclude that the detained alumina was prohibited.

Official ESDM policy record

ESDM meeting minutes confirm that Indonesia’s REE export-verification framework is not yet complete

Meeting date: 21 July 2026. The Directorate General of Minerals and Coal convened ministries, customs, testing institutions and mineral-surveyor companies to align their treatment of associated minerals, including rare-earth elements, in processed and refined mineral products.

No automatic reclassificationThe presence of REEs does not automatically turn a product into an REE product when the elements have not been separated, are not used independently and do not have standalone economic value as a traded commodity.
No minimum threshold yetThe meeting recorded that no regulation currently establishes the minimum REE content that should trigger a specific treatment during export verification.
No universal test methodTesting must reflect the mineralogy, commodity and target element. XRF/XRD are used for principal minerals, while REEs generally require ICP-OES or ICP-MS and commodity-specific sample preparation.

Indonesia is becoming two markets at once

Indonesia’s refinery build-out is creating a looser regional alumina balance, while its primary aluminum market remains comparatively tight until new smelters reach stable commercial production.

02 · Indonesia Market Outlook

From raw-ore exporter to regional processing hub

Indonesia’s 2023 raw-bauxite export ban changed the location of value creation. By 2026, the central question is no longer whether refining capacity will be built, but whether bauxite, power, logistics and downstream demand can support all of it economically.

Alumina: volume growth, margin compression

SMM’s 2026 research places Indonesia on a path toward roughly 8 Mt/y of alumina capacity. The same research shows Indonesian alumina prices having fallen sharply from the late-2024 peak, leaving new refineries dependent on low-cost captive bauxite, energy efficiency and secure offtake rather than unusually high refining margins.

Current company intelligence confirms that the capacity is concentrated in a small number of very large assets. WHW is established at 2 Mt/y. Bintan stated that it had reached 4 Mt/y by November 2025. Borneo Alumina is targeting 1 Mt in 2026, with Phase II planned to lift the complex to 2 Mt/y.

Primary aluminum: additions are real but uneven

Huachin’s 500 kt/y line is the clearest large-scale addition covered by the trip. Inalum’s existing 275 kt/y smelter remains constrained by hydropower availability and cannot operate as a flexible swing producer. Bintan’s planned smelter and Inalum’s West Kalimantan project are important, but their tonnes should enter market balances only as commissioning milestones are reached.

Indonesia therefore has a split balance: alumina can become surplus before domestic primary aluminum does. This supports exports of alumina and imports or regional arbitrage in selected primary products at the same time.

SMM market view: The refining surplus is not automatically bearish for every Indonesian producer. Integrated assets with captive ore and power can remain competitive after prices fall, while non-integrated projects face rapid margin compression if domestic bauxite prices rise.

03 · Regulatory Framework

Rules that directly change the trade

Indonesia’s aluminum chain is governed by overlapping mining, benchmark-pricing, export and verification rules. The practical consequence is that a cargo can be economically viable but still be delayed by quota, purity, surveyor or port-compliance issues.

Raw bauxite export ban
Effective June 2023

Raw bauxite must be processed domestically. This is the policy foundation for the refinery build-out and removes Indonesian raw ore from normal seaborne supply.

RKAB + SIMBARA

Annual RKAB approvals govern mine production volumes. SIMBARA connects approvals, production reporting, royalties and export documentation, making feed availability and compliance traceable at mine level.

Law No. 2/2025

The fourth amendment to the Mineral and Coal Mining Law broadens access to mining permits while tightening production discipline, reporting and rehabilitation obligations.

Kepmen ESDM No. 144.K/2026
Bauxite HPM

The revised bauxite benchmark is calculated in US$/WMT and explicitly includes alumina grade, reactive silica and moisture: HPM = [(1.5% × HMA Aluminum) ± Al₂O₃ adjustment − R-SiO₂ adjustment] × (1 − moisture). Reactive silica above 2% attracts a deduction, capped at US$3.5/DMT, and moisture converts the reference to wet tonnes.

Permendag No. 22/2023
as amended by No. 6/2026

Smelter-grade alumina below 98% Al₂O₃ and chemical-grade alumina below 90% Al₂O₃ are listed in the prohibited-export schedule, subject to the regulation’s exceptions.

Permendag No. 23/2023
as amended by Nos. 5 and 12/2026

Compliant-grade alumina sits in the controlled-export framework and requires the applicable exporter documentation and surveyor verification, including the Laporan Surveyor (LS).

PP No. 24/2026

This creates a broader governance framework for strategic natural-resource exports. It is relevant policy background, but its initial implementation covers coal, palm oil and ferroalloys. Alumina or rare-earth products would require a further designation before this framework could be treated as directly applicable to them.

A primary aluminum smelter then converts alumina into metal through electrolytic reduction, making electricity the dominant operating-cost variable.

12 · Cost & Logistics Assessment

The cost curve is built before the refinery gate

For alumina, bauxite quality and power determine margin. For primary aluminum, electricity dominates. Dedicated infrastructure lowers normal cost but creates concentrated permit and outage exposure.

Bauxite feed
SMM tracked Indonesian bauxite around US$28–32/t FOB in early 2026. At an indicative 2.4:1 ore-to-alumina ratio, this represents about US$67–77/t of bauxite cost per tonne of alumina before inland logistics and quality adjustments.
Ore quality
The revised HPM formula rewards Al₂O₃ above 47% and penalises reactive silica above 2%, while moisture converts the benchmark to WMT. Two cargoes with the same headline alumina grade can therefore have materially different economic value.
Refining conversion
The Bayer process requires caustic soda, steam/power, maintenance and residue management. SMM’s chain framework uses roughly 14 GJ of energy per tonne of alumina as an industry reference.
Primary smelting
SMM uses about 13,500 kWh of electricity and roughly two tonnes of alumina per tonne of primary aluminum. Electricity is therefore the decisive competitive variable for Huachin and Inalum.
Port and freight
WHW and Bintan benefit from dedicated jetties; Bintan also benefits from proximity to Singapore routes. Borneo Alumina sells FOB. Inalum has integrated port and warehouse facilities. Actual advantage depends on loading rate, draft, vessel size, queue and permit continuity.
Carbon
Inalum’s hydropower improves carbon positioning. Coal-based refinery and smelter parks can have lower direct cash cost but higher embedded emissions and greater exposure to CBAM-sensitive customers.

14 · Key Risks

Execution risk is now larger than concept risk

The downstreaming strategy is established. What can change the market is the speed and reliability with which ore, power, ports and new potlines perform.

Bauxite approval and reserve risk

RKAB volumes may lag the feed requirement of the growing refinery fleet. Longer term, the reserve-life debate could produce tighter approvals, delayed projects or greater reliance on imported ore.

Alumina oversupply

Several large refineries can reach stable production before equivalent smelting demand is online, pressuring FOB alumina margins and raising competition for long-term offtake.

Power availability

Hydropower limits Inalum’s flexibility, while thermal assets face coal, reliability and carbon risks. A smelter announcement without a credible power solution should receive a low confidence score.

Port and permit disruption

The WHW jetty event illustrates how a permit issue can interrupt or threaten loadings even without stopping refinery operations.

REE-related export verification

The July 2026 detention shows that detectable REEs can trigger customs attention even though no published threshold currently links trace REE content to the export status of compliant alumina. Repeated testing requirements could lengthen LS lead times, reduce prompt availability and create demurrage exposure without changing annual nameplate supply.

Data visibility

SEZ trade flows and captive transfers can make normal customs series incomplete. Vessel tracking and destination-country imports are needed to estimate real supply.

Commissioning slippage

New potlines and refinery phases often reach mechanical completion before stable commercial output. Announced capacity should be discounted until operating evidence is available.

Carbon and market access

Coal-based Indonesian metal can be cost-competitive in Asia but may face a widening disadvantage in CBAM-sensitive markets relative to hydropower or recycled material.

15 · Trading Implications

What to do with the information

The commercial value of the report lies in converting capacity, operating conditions, regulation and logistics into market variables that can be monitored and traded.

Alumina FOB Indonesia

Track merchant availability, not nameplate—and distinguish physical supply from export-ready supply.

WHW and Bintan are the main export-relevant assets. Deduct long-term offtake, captive transfers and commissioning losses before estimating spot supply. Then apply a second filter for cargo documentation, LS issuance and any additional REE testing. Borneo Alumina should be modelled primarily as captive supply with a possible interim export surplus.

Primary aluminum

Near-term domestic tightness can coexist with alumina surplus.

Inalum’s power constraint and domestic priority support local premiums. Huachin increases supply, but its external saleable product and LME qualification must be confirmed before using full nameplate in a regional balance.

MJP-linked business

Define the premium precisely.

For Inalum or imported metal discussions, confirm whether “QMJP” means the settled quarterly Japanese premium, a spot reference or an average. Also define quotation period, freight, Incoterm, currency and any VAP premium.

Bauxite procurement

Value quality through the new HPM formula.

Reactive silica and moisture now directly affect the benchmark. Traders should quote ore using Al₂O₃, R-SiO₂, moisture, delivery basis and the applicable HMA month, not a single nominal grade.

Billet

Separate primary and recycled billet.

IAA’s secondary billet competes through delivered cost and local service. Compare it with imported primary billet after alloy specification, diameter, extrusion performance, credit and logistics—not simply LME plus one generic premium.

Physical risk

Build event triggers around ports and regulation.

Jetty sealing, RKAB approvals, smelter commissioning and LS issuance remain discrete event risks. For REEs, the official trigger list now includes publication of a threshold, a national test method, KAN-accredited laboratory coverage, a written surveyor instruction and any transitional treatment for cargoes whose LS remains pending.
